## Role & Responsibilities
CHRONO HUNT ranked 274th in Enjoyment and 383rd in Creativity out of 10.5k submissions to the GMTK Game Jam 2026 (community vote).

It's a solo web game in pure JavaScript, built with Claude in 3 days of the 7-day jam in July 2026. Every idea and mechanic was new for the jam.

## Gameplay
- You start on a 30-second clock, and destroying targets buys time back.
- Freeze time, then touch frozen enemies to make them explode.
- Play on a keyboard (WASD + SPACE) or with a mobile touch stick.
- A global top-20 leaderboard runs on Cloudflare Workers.
