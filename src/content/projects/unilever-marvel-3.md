## Role & Responsibilities
I led the full project with a team of 9, from November 2024 to mid-January 2025. It's a standalone PC and PCVR interactive training app for Unilever, and Unilever reported 300+ distinct trainings run by its employees.

**What I built:** the user navigation, and a 400-part process flow visualization with automated pipe tracing.

**What I led:** interaction design, UI/UX decisions, and the workflow and process structure.

The public demo, "Unilever Marvel 3 Virtual Training", is linked above.
