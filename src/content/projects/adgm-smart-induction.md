## Role & Responsibilities
Lead the development of the smart induction platform by closely working with stakeholders, managing the team of developers and designers. The project delivers a virtual tour of 7 individual office floors fully explorable with guided pathways. 

## Learnings
One of the key challenges of this project was to deliver a large scale 3D environment over the web with low load times. With efficiently using addressables and systematically loading-unloading environmental chunks only when needed resulted in a <b>load time reduction by 30%</b>.
