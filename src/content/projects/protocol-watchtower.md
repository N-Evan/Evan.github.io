## Role & Responsibilities
Each of the 10 minigames went from concept to an accepted, playable proof of concept in 2–3 days. The final versions took longer while the art assets and visual direction were confirmed.

I set the project's development direction and made the key architectural decisions. I also directed all 10 minigames, each with its own features and mechanics, through group ideation and decision-making, and they were created with Claude.

Protocol Watchtower is a cyber-security awareness game for UNICEF, with a full 2D cutscene storyline and real voiceovers. We built it from April to June 2026 with a team of 7, and it shipped on Google Play and the App Store.
