This is where I'll be writing about gameplay programming, level design, and
the messy parts of shipping games. Expect post-mortems, breakdowns of systems
I'm proud (or embarrassed) of, and the occasional rant about feel.

## What you can expect

- **Post-mortems** of my shipped projects — what worked, what didn't, what I'd
  do differently next time.
- **Tooling notes** on Unity, Photon, Blender, and small open-source utilities
  I build along the way.
- **Design breakdowns** of mechanics from games I love.

## What this isn't

A list of tutorials or hot takes about the industry. There's plenty of that
elsewhere. This is the *workshop* — the unfinished bits, the experiments,
the lessons earned the hard way.

If anything resonates, [send a transmission](/#comms). Always up for a chat.
